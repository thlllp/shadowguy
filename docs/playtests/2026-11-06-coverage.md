## Balance / feel (not defects)

- **Rival pressure is very low.** A passive corp with six districts and no
  garrisons lost its first district on day 52 and its last on day 104, while
  accumulating **14,175eb** doing nothing. A player who ignores corp mode for
  months is safe; a corp defeat needs a very long soak.

  resting at a 10eb lodging district, because `rest()` charges `min(cost, cash)`.
- **Critical failures on watch checks are rare** at skill value 2 against
  difficulty 12–13; the “blown contract” consequence exists but is unlikely to
  be seen in ordinary play.
