## Balance / feel

- Sabotage is offered on territories whose Security is already 0; selecting it spent an
  operative and an AP for a result that cannot improve (Security stayed 0). Consider
  hiding the row (or warning) at Security 0 — the TUI's own `sabotage_targets` does not
  check the modifier.
- Corp-only captures look hard to land with the Academy's one-operative batches: two
  attack attempts with equal force are coin-flips, and training another batch costs six
  more days while rivals reinforce. That is design pacing, not a defect, but it leaves
  capture untested.
