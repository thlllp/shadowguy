## Findings

### [P2] Saving inside a fight and loading discards the encounter
- Seed: 20261106
- Screen: CorpMapScreen → TollScreen → CombatScreen → QuitMenu → LoadMenu
- Steps:
  1. Accept Dolman's heist at Netherworks; run it at Garnet (this leaves Ghostwire standing at −2).
  2. Travel to Pressgang and back to Garnet until the corp security encounter fires.
  3. Refuse the 130eb fine → CombatScreen (`state.is_over == False`, log shows the first exchange).
  4. `q` → Save Game. Save succeeds while the fight is live.
  5. `q` → Load Game → the freshly written slot.
- Expected: either saving is refused during a fight, or the encounter is restored (or at minimum the player is warned that the fight will be dropped).
- Actual: the game reopens CorpMapScreen with the same health/location and no encounter. A player can save at 1 HP in a losing fight, load, and walk away — the fight, and the refused toll, are gone.
- Evidence:
  - Before save: `screen=CombatScreen`, `state.is_over False`, `hp 26`
  - After save: `saved mid-fight is_over False`
  - After load: `screen=CorpMapScreen hp 26`, no fight state on the app
  - The save bundle (`app.save_run`) has no combat/encounter key; `load_state` always reopens CorpMapScreen.
- Repro: yes, 2/2 (two separate saves from the same fight).

### [P3] The Rest key exists but is undiscoverable (footer clipped, manual silent)
- Seed: 20261106 (also 20261107)
- Screen: CorpMapScreen / CorpScreen
- Steps: begin a run, read the Footer and the field manual's CONTROLS list, then press `R`.
- Expected: the key that rests is listed where players look.
- Actual: `Binding("R", "rest_action", "Rest")` does work — it rests 8h (runner) / to 6 AM next day (corp), verified — but at 80 and 100 columns the runner map's Footer is already full and truncates before the `R Rest` tile, and the field manual's CONTROLS section never mentions `R`. The `#rest_button` itself is `can_focus=False`, so it is mouse-only. (An earlier draft of this report claimed there was no key at all; that was wrong — the binding is present.)
- Repro: yes, 2/2 (key works; hint not visible).

### [P3] Selecting an out-of-district Job/Legwork row silently no-ops
- Seed: 20261106
- Screen: CorpMapScreen → Jobs (`j`) / Legwork (`l`)
- Steps: accept a job whose target is another district, open Legwork (or Jobs) while standing elsewhere, select the only row — its label already says "travel to Garnet".

## Balance / feel

- Traveling two in-game days without resting drove fatigue to 3 (−3 to every stat) before the heist; the heist's checks then failed repeatedly. The mechanic is documented, but the cost lands hard on a long fast-travel route (Garnet is ~2 days from the western districts).
- The heist paid 325eb and 10xp with no deadline; one such job funds a full loadout. Fine for a first job, but worth checking against the early-run economy.
- The cheapest cyberware (700eb) is still unaffordable after one heist (425eb). A first-session ripperdoc visit is browse-only.
