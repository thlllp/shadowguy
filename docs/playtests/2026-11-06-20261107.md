Date: 2026-11-06
Seed: **20261107** (Corp-only, Ironclad Dynamics unless noted)
Build: headless `run_test()` at 100x40 and 80x24; `saves.SAVE_DIR=/tmp/opencode/allie/saves`

## Verdict

Corp session covering expansion, Academy training/grants, tech research, the repeatable
Logistics Optimization project, save/load, operative training and tasking, and 120 days
of rival AI. Two 80x24 layout defects found. **Findings: 2 P2, 1 P3. No crashes.**

## Findings

### [P2] Corp ledger's Logistics / Research Facility lines are clipped and unscrollable at 80x24
- Seed: 20261107
- Screen: CorpMapScreen (corp-only map mode)
- Steps: New Game → Corp → Ironclad Dynamics at 80x24; expand once, train a research
  assistant, rest a few days so the ledger has real numbers; read the bottom ledger.
- Expected: the ledger `76f58e7` claims to keep on screen shows supply and research
  status at the documented 80x24 minimum.
- Actual: `#corp_info` is `Region(x=0, y=20, width=80, height=3)` with 4 wrapped content
  lines. Only these render:
  - `Ironclad Dynamics — 1550eb — 12.0rp — ... — Day 6`
  - `Territories (7): Marlstone, Diesel, ...`
  `Logistics: 7/14 districts supplied` and `Research Facility: tier 1 (1rp/day base), ...`
  never appear. `virtual_size == 3x80` (no overflow), `can_focus == False`, no tab target
  reaches it — there is no way to scroll to them. At 100x40 all four lines render.
- Why the regression test misses it: `test_corp_only_map_keeps_actions_and_ledger_on_screen_at_80x24`
  asserts `"Research Facility" in #corp_info.content`; the content string contains it,
  but the rendered screen does not.
- Repro: yes, 2/2 at 80x24; 2/2 visible at 100x40.

### [P2] At 80x24 the Corp tab's Academy / Research / Surveillance panels sit below the viewport, yet are keyboard-focusable and spend resources
- Seed: 20261107
- Screen: CorpMapScreen (corp-only) → Corp category
- Steps:
  1. New Game → Corp → Ironclad Dynamics at 80x24, open the Corp tab.
  2. Tab until focus reaches `academy_list` (the list is not visible).
  3. Press Enter on the `academy_grant` row.
- Expected: the panels are on screen, or the screen scrolls to them, or focus cannot enter
  widgets outside the viewport.
- Actual: `#activities` autosizes to its rows and occupies `y=8..26`; `#academy_panel` is
  `Region(x=0, y=28, width=80, height=9)`, `#research_panel` at `y=37`, `#surveillance_panel`
  at `y=43` — all outside the 24-row viewport. The Screen's virtual size is 24 (it cannot
  scroll). The invisible `Research grant` row is still focusable and fires: cash
  2000→1750, RP 0→2, AP 2→1. The screen text never contains "Research grant".
- Evidence: widget regions above; before/after `(2000, 0, 2)` → `(1750, 2, 1)`.
- Repro: yes, 2/2. At 100x40 the Academy panel is visible but the Surveillance panel still
  starts at y=41 (below the viewport).

## Balance / feel

- **Logistics Optimization is a very long-term sink.** First level costs 200rp; the entire
  published tech tree tops out at 50rp. With 1 research assistant and two Academy grants a
  day the corp reached 200rp on around day 70 and bought level 1 on day 94 (RP 266→66,
  level 0→1; the next row correctly reads `400rp — level 1 → 2`). DESIGN.md already says
  this is "Not balance-simulated yet"; a normal session will never see level 1.
- **The Corp action list is flooded.** On day 13 the activities list held 253 `Gather intel`
  rows (one per territory on the 260-district board), 35 sabotage rows, 2 tail rows and 7
  reinforce rows. At 80x24 only ~15 rows are visible; deploy/attack rows are buried among
  map-wide intel entries. It works with scrolling, but it is not a usable action surface.
- **Rival AI vs. the player is very quiet on this seed.** Over 120 days the rivals made
  480 faction actions; the first attack came on day 87, and by day 121 there were 8 attacks
  and 7 captures — all AI-vs-AI. The player's 6 districts were never attacked once.
  Expansion accounted for 1,167 actions. If the intent is that a corp-only run should feel
  contested before day 80, this seed does not deliver it.
- Expand → train (6 days) → deploy is the only army loop reachable here; attacking a rival
  never became possible because no rival territory bordered the player's 6 districts by
  day 13 (and still none by day 121 in the soak).

## Coverage

Exercised: Corp selection; map/corp tabs; expand onto neutral ground (AP + cash, owned
districts 6→7, income 160→230/day after Worker Surveillance); train research assistant
(3 days) and operative (6 days); Academy Research grant (+2rp, −250eb, 1 AP; blocked while
a batch trains); day ticks (income, RP, AP refill, training completion, sightings);
Research Tree (Worker Surveillance 10rp, `researched` set, income bonus applied);
repeatable Logistics Optimization purchase (level 1, 200rp, next cost 400rp);
save → load with a pending recruit (cash/RP/AP/recruit/districts/day all restored);
operative deploy (garrison 0→1, operative consumed, AP spent); tasking — tail a runner
and gather intel (2 operatives, tasking 2, both return next day); 120 days of rival AI
(8 attacks, 7 AI-vs-AI captures after day 87); 80x24 and 100x40 layouts.

Not reached: player-vs-rival attacks (no adjacent rival target), sabotage execution
(rows present, no AP left after tail+intel), corp defeat (player held all 6 districts
for 120 days), runner/corp switching or takeover, 80x24 Corp tab with the panels scrolled
into view (there is no scroll path).
